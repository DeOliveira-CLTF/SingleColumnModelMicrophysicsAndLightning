                                             is that its definition on a fixed isobar makes it poorly suited to the study of global warming. To see why, note
                                    that lightning is caused by charging that takes place in the mixed-phase region of clouds (Williams et al.,

                              (Romps, 2014), cloud anvils (Hartmann & Larson, 2002), the tropopause (DWJ. Thompson et al., 2017; Seeley
                                       et al., 2019), and radiative cooling (Jeevanjee & Romps, 2018).
